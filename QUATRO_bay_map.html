Opening

  1. Squeeze the day's citrus. Fill rail bottles 1, 2 and 4. Bin yesterday's.
  2. Check F1A top shelf. Refill simple, apple-pear, vanilla and lychee from the F1A bottom shelf.
  3. Check F1B. Anything low or past its day dot gets batched now, not mid-service.
  4. Check F1C top shelf. Refill empty squeeze bottles from the F1C bottom shelf.
  5. Fresh delivery into F2A. Citrus below, everything else above.
  6. Fill the ice bin at station 2.

Mid-service

  1. Rail bottle empty: swap from the F1A bottom shelf.
  2. Squeeze bottle empty: refill from the F1C bottom shelf.
  3. Anything past its day dot comes out immediately.
  4. Refill the ice bin at the midpoint.
NOBODY OPENS THE DRY STORE DURING SERVICE. Dry is backup only. If the fridge is empty of something, the drink comes off the board until close.

Closing

  1. Day-dot everything opened today.
  2. Move tomorrow's stock from DRY into F1A, F1B and F1C to chill overnight, ginger beer and soda included.
  3. Bin the day's citrus and cut garnish.
  4. Wipe F1C. Purees do not sit dirty overnight.
