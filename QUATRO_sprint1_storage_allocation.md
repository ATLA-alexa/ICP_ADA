## READ THIS BEFORE YOU SET THE SHELVES

Three tiers at the 297 mm pitch does not survive the arithmetic, and it is worth sixty seconds with a tape before anyone orders bottles.

| Check | Two tiers at 297 | Three tiers at 297 |
|---|---|---|
| Cavity height needed | 297 + 297 + 6 = **600 mm** | 297 x 3 + shelves = **891 mm** |
| Volume that implies | 1.270 x 0.420 x 0.600 = **320 L** | **482 L** |
| Against the plate rating | **320 L, exact match** | rating says 320 L |
| Against the 851 mm external height | fits, 251 mm for base and compressor | **891 mm of cavity inside an 851 mm cabinet** |

**So one of two things is true, and the tape tells you which:**

- **Cavity measures 600 mm.** Then there are two shelf positions at 297 mm, and the three bands on the elevation are two shelves plus the cabinet floor.
- **Cavity measures 600 mm but three shelves are fitted.** Then the pitch is 600 / 3 = **200 mm**, and a 270 mm bottle will not stand in any of them. In that case pull one shelf out of F1A and F1B so the bottles fit, and keep the third shelf in **F1C only**, where the tubs are 180 mm and clear a 200 mm tier easily.

**Measure once: cavity floor to ceiling.** Everything below is written so it does not matter which answer you get, because **every item v2 adds to a middle tier is backup or overflow**. If the third tier is not there, nothing on the menu loses its home. You lose depth of backup, not a drink.

## THE DAILY CHECKLIST

**OPENING**
1. Squeeze the day's citrus. Fill rail bottles 1, 2 and 4. Bin yesterday's.
2. F1A top: front row live, **back row full**. Refill from the F1A bottom tier.
3. F1B top: anything low or past its day dot is batched now, not mid-service.
4. F1C top: refill empty squeeze bottles from the F1C bottom tier.
5. Fresh delivery into F2A. Citrus below, everything else above.
6. Fill the ice bin at station 2.
7. **The backup rows should be full at open and should not move during service. A backup row empty at open is last night's closing miss, not today's problem.**

**MID-SERVICE**
1. Rail bottle empty: take the backup from the row behind it in F1A top.
2. Squeeze bottle empty: take the double in F1C top, or refill from F1C bottom.
3. Anything past its day dot comes out immediately.
4. Refill the ice bin at the midpoint.
5. **Nobody opens the dry store during service.** If the fridge is out of it, the drink comes off the board until close.

**CLOSING**
1. Day-dot everything opened today.
2. Move tomorrow's ginger beer and soda from DRY into the F1B bottom tier to chill overnight.
3. **Rebuild the backup rows in F1A top and F1C top from tonight's prep**, so tomorrow opens with backup already in place.
4. Bin the day's citrus and cut garnish.
5. Wipe F1C. Purees do not sit dirty overnight.
